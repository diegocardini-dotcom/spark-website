An Enrolled Agent (EA) is a tax professional licensed directly by the U.S. Department of the Treasury. It is the highest credential the IRS awards, and it authorizes the holder to represent any taxpayer, in any state, before the IRS. In practice, that means Juan can not only prepare your return but also speak and act on your behalf if the IRS has questions or opens an audit.
